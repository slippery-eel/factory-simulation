# Orchestrating the Living Factory (Scale & Logistics)

## Objective

This case study assesses your ability to define a product strategy and industrial roadmap that transitions a manual "human-on-a-cart" operation into a high-throughput, software-defined factory.

You will demonstrate your skills in:
- Industrial Engineering (IE)
- Facility layout
- Throughput optimization
- Labor economics
- Leveraging AI to manage the logistics of 500,000 biological units in motion

---

# Scenario

Ohalo is scaling its "Seed Production Factory."

Unlike a standard warehouse, the "inventory" is alive and must hit strict biological deadlines to remain viable.

---

## The Current State

### Logistics
- All movement is manual
- Technicians push rolling carts between:
  - Buffer Warehouse
  - Pollination Zone
- Each cart carries 50 plants

### Transition Leak
- Typical pollination session:
  - 5 plants
  - 45 minutes total
- Of that:
  - 15 minutes (33%) is dead time spent on:
    - Prep
    - Moving carts

### Scale Cliff
- Projected volume:
  - 500,000 plants/week within 6–9 months
- Without automation:
  - ~$1M labor spend
  - Physical gridlock risk

### Biological Clock
- Flowering/pollination plants have a strict 48-hour viability window
- If not moved from storage to light + irrigation within 48 hours:
  - Breeding cycle risks failure

---

## The Friction Point

### Labor Waste
~33% of highly skilled labor is spent on simple material movement.

### Biological Risk
Priority flowering plants must be moved and processed within a strict 48-hour window or breeding cycles become at risk.

---

# The Task (Time-Boxed: 2 Hours)

---

## 1. AI-Powered Opportunity Analysis & ROI (45 mins)

Analyze labor and material movement efficiency opportunities and risks.

### Trade-off Analysis
Compare ROI of:
- Automating a workstation
  - Robotic pollinator
  - Automated cutting station
- Automating logistics
  - Goods-to-Person systems
  - Conveyors
  - Autonomous movement systems
  - Systems eliminating the 33% movement leak

### Output
Identify:
- Which investment moves the needle most on total facility throughput
- Recommended roadmap:
  - Build vs buy
  - Automation sequencing

---

## 2. Industrial Layout & Material Flow (45 mins)

Design the physical layout and flow for 500,000 plants/week.

### Industrial Engineering Focus
Create a high-density layout for the Buffer Warehouse.

Requirements:
- Organize 500k units efficiently
- Ensure urgent flowering plants are always accessible

### Ergonomics
Transition from:
- "Technician walks to plant"

To:
- "System delivers plant to technician"

### Logical Leverage
Describe logic used to:
- Minimize travel distance
- Optimize total facility movement

---

## 3. PRD: The "Facility Orchestrator" (30 mins)

Write a 1–2 page PRD focused on the top opportunity identified in the ROI analysis.

The PRD should include:
- Hardware automation
- Software orchestration layer

### Key Feature
#### Biological Priority Queue
Automatically triggers movement tasks based on biological timing deadlines.

### Bits-to-Atoms Synchronization
Define how software ensures:
- Digital record of a "Cross" (pollination)
- Remains perfectly synced with:
  - Physical plant
  - Location
  - State transitions
  - Automated movement systems
